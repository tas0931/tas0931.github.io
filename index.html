Research experience

  • Contrastive ECG–Report Alignment on PTB-XL: Measuring the Gap Between Learned Representations and Prompt-Based Diagnosis First author

    2026 · Accepted at the AgIA4BioHealth workshop, IEEE BIBM 2026
    Tasbir Ahmed, Roza Akter · BRAC University

    Before training, I audited the PTB-XL report text and found about 59% of the reports in German and about 55% exact duplicates, so I built English pseudo reports from the 71 standard diagnostic statements. I trained a two tower contrastive model with a patient wise split and evaluated the same frozen encoder in two ways, by text prompts and by a linear probe.

    I defined the difference between the two as a retrieval gap and proposed a retrieval efficiency measure, which showed that prompts reach only about a third of the diagnostic information the encoder holds. The gap was largest for conduction disturbance, which I traced to a vocabulary mismatch between prompts and training text. Trained on a single 8 GB GPU.

    Prompt AUPRC 0.417 (chance 0.259)Linear probe 0.751Retrieval efficiency 0.32PyTorchBio ClinicalBERTContrastive learning

  • Undergraduate thesis: Multimodal Grading of Nonalcoholic Fatty Liver Disease from Ultrasound and Clinical Data First author

    2026 · BRAC University · Defended and approved, October 2026
    Supervisors: Dr. Md. Golam Rabiul Alam, Rafeed Rahman, Shoaib Ahmed Dipu

    A model that grades NAFLD as normal, steatosis or advanced from B mode ultrasound and 11 clinical measurements, on a public dataset of 84 patients. Each image is enhanced with bilateral filtering and CLAHE, encoded by an attention gated encoder that produces a spatial attention map with every prediction, and fused with a clinical MLP. I designed the thesis end to end: the pipeline, the methodology and the code.

    Accuracy rose from 46.2% for an image only ResNet50 to 69.2% with clinical fusion and 92.3% with the attention gated encoder, ahead of every literature baseline reimplemented on the same data. Under leakage free stratified fivefold cross validation, which I report as the reliable estimate, the model reached 71.5% ± 11.8%. A controlled ablation showed the fused model beat either modality alone.
